Scanning and local storage
Folio lets you scan, import, edit, recognize text and store documents without creating an account. Folio accesses the camera to capture pages and opens photos or files you select. Scanned images, PDFs, extracted text, signatures and editing preferences may be stored in the app’s private storage. Core scan editing and text recognition run on your device. On supported Android devices, Google Play services provides the automatic scanner and may download its components. Optional app lock uses Android device authentication. Folio does not receive your fingerprint data or device PIN.

Export and sharing
If you export, print or share a PDF, Folio sends the selected file to the folder, print service or app you choose. Copies outside Folio are controlled by you or by the recipient and its service.

Accounts and cloud services
When account services are available and you create an account, Folio’s server processes your name, email address, password hash and session information to manage your account. If billing is available and you make a purchase, Folio may process purchase identifiers, verification tokens and subscription status with Google Play.

If you sign in and enable cloud backup, Folio uploads eligible final PDFs and basic document metadata to the configured server and storage provider. Scans created in private ID mode are excluded from automatic backup. Turning backup off stops future automatic uploads. It does not delete previously backed-up files. Cloud PDF tools may upload an entire selected file for the requested task after you consent. Cloud processing and backup require a working connection to Folio’s services.

Optional Google Drive backup
On Android builds where Drive backup is configured, you can connect a Google account separately from your Folio account. Folio stores the connected account identifier and email on your device. Google handles sign-in and authorization; Folio does not receive your Google password. When enabled, Folio sends eligible PDFs, their filenames and integrity checksums to the Folio/Documents folder in your Google Drive. These copies use your Google storage quota. Private ID scans are excluded. Disconnecting stops new transfers. You can delete visible backups directly in Google Drive. Legacy hidden backups can be removed through Drive’s connected-app controls.

Retention and deletion
Local documents remain in Folio until removed or app data is cleared. Items moved to Trash can be restored. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local files, while copies exported elsewhere remain in those locations.

When account services are available, go to Profile and select Delete cloud account to request deletion. The server disables access and queues deletion of account data and cloud files.
