App name
Folio: PDF Scanner & Editor

Short description
Scan documents, clean up pages, and edit, sign or organize PDFs on your phone.

Full description
Turn receipts, notes, forms and paperwork into PDFs with Folio. Scan with your camera, import photos, or open a PDF you already have. Review each page before saving and keep your documents organized on your phone.

Scan and clean up pages
• Capture a single page or build a multipage scan.
• Use automatic edge detection and cropping on supported Android devices.
• Retake a page, adjust its corners, rotate it, or change the page order.
• Choose Clean Color, grayscale or black-and-white filters. Fine-tune brightness and contrast with a visible page preview.

Work with PDFs
• Merge PDFs, extract selected pages, or reorder and rotate pages.
• Add page numbers or drag text watermarks into position.
• Draw a handwritten signature, place it on the page and save a signed copy.
• Compress a PDF to an image-based copy with an optional KB or MB target. Results depend on the source file.
• Recognize English or Hindi text from saved documents on your device.

Save and find your documents
• Save a PDF in Folio or export a copy to a folder you choose.
• Organize files in folders and find them by title or recognized text.
• Share a copy using your phone’s share menu.

Core scanning and local PDF tools work without a Folio account. Automatic scanning may require Google Play services to download scanner components on supported devices.
